Known exceptions

One exception, kept on purpose from the Vanilla framework and noted on the token it affects:

  • Caution-coloured text (--orca-caution) has 3.0:1 contrast in light and paper. Never use it alone: add the warning icon and set the text at 16px or more.

ORCA also departs from Vanilla in three places: a darker focus colour in light and paper (#0f6dcc, at least 3.9:1 instead of 2.3–3.0:1), brand fills adjusted to 4.5:1, and a switch that animates in 0.333s instead of 0.5s.

What remains yours

  • Writing alt text that describes what matters in each image.
  • Keeping the heading outline in order and one <h1> per page.
  • Moving focus to the right place after an action: the error summary after a failed submit, the opener when a dialog closes.
  • Announcing results that appear without a page load in a role="status" region.
  • Testing with a keyboard and a screen reader, and with people who use them.
