Known exceptions

Kept from Vanilla framework on purpose, and documented on the tokens they affect:

  • --orca-caution as text is 3.0:1 in light and paper. Never set caution text on its own: pair it with the warning icon, at 16px or more.

Departures ORCA made from Vanilla for accessibility: the light and paper focus colour is darker (#0f6dcc, at least 3.9:1 on every ground instead of 2.3–3.0:1), brand fills are derived to reach 4.5:1, and the switch animates in 0.333s instead of 0.5s.

What remains yours

  • Writing alt text that describes what matters in each image.
  • Keeping the heading outline in order and one <h1> per page.
  • Moving focus sensibly after actions: to the error summary after a failed submit, back to the opener when a dialog closes.
  • Announcing results that appear without a page load in a role="status" region.
  • Testing with a keyboard and a screen reader, and with people who use them.
