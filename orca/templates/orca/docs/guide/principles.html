Sources

  • Laws of UX, Jon Yablonski, 2nd edition, 2024: ten observations about how people perceive and decide.
  • Practical UI, Adham Dannaway, 2nd edition, 2024: concrete rules for colour, layout, type, writing, buttons and forms. ORCA uses the rule identifiers of the ORCA study guide (F01, C03, R14…).
  • The ORCA Design System Guide: the inventory and the five levels, after Brad Frost's Atomic Design.

These are guidelines, not laws of nature. When two seem to disagree (familiar versus distinctive, fast versus deliberate), the element's “How to use” records the choice ORCA made.
